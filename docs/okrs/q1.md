# Tonism — OKRs Q1

**Quarter window:** 2026-05-06 → 2026-08-06
**Status:** Draft
**Last reviewed:** 2026-05-06

These are the first quarterly OKRs for Tonism. They are derived directly from the [definition of success](../specs/product-architecture.md#definition-of-success): every KR defends a specific failure mode in one of the [product layers](../specs/product-architecture.md#product-layers).

The scope is deliberately small — two objectives, no community/adoption objective. Per the [strategic intent](../specs/product-architecture.md#strategic-intent), Tonism is a personal tool shared as OSS; adoption is not a goal, so loading the OKRs with adoption KRs would distort priorities.

---

## Objective 1 — Prove a glitch-free real-time guitar signal path

This is the foundation. Every other product effort is blocked on it. If O1 fails, nothing else matters; if O1 succeeds, the project has a real spine to build on.

| ID | Key result | Defends layer | Target date |
|---|---|---|---|
| KR1.1 | Measured round-trip latency < 10 ms on the dev machine, with the measurement method documented | Capture, Render | End of week 1 (2026-05-13) |
| KR1.2 | Zero buffer underruns during a continuous 5-minute session, logged by an in-app xrun counter | Capture | End of week 1 (2026-05-13) |
| KR1.3 | Crash-free 30-minute stress session with parameter changes throughout | Capture, Tone state, Control surface | End of week 2 (2026-05-20) |
| KR1.4 | Author plays a full song end-to-end through Tonism without a perceived glitch (the success signal — binary KR) | All | End of week 2 (2026-05-20) |

KR1.1–KR1.4 are also the [MVP acceptance criteria](../specs/mvp/spec.md#acceptance-criteria). The MVP exists to satisfy O1.

---

## Objective 2 — Reach a tone the author would actually gig with

O2 is post-MVP. Defining it now is what justifies leaving tone work *out* of the MVP — without O2 on paper, MVP scope would drift toward tone polish and lose the "prove the path first" focus.

| ID | Key result | Defends layer | Target date |
|---|---|---|---|
| KR2.1 | NAM profile loading working with at least one curated profile audible end-to-end | Signal chain | 2026-06-30 |
| KR2.2 | At least 3 distinct usable tones available (e.g. clean, crunch, lead), author-judged | Signal chain, Tone state | 2026-07-15 |
| KR2.3 | Author self-reports "I'd gig this" after a one-week daily-use trial | All | 2026-08-06 |

KR2.3 is the gate to closing Q1: until the author would actually gig with it, the quarter is not won.

---

## Deliberately not in this OKR set

- **No community/adoption objective.** Stars, contributors, and downloads are not Tonism's measure of success.
- **No plugin (VST3/CLAP) export objective.** Standalone-first; plugin export is a future quarter's question once the standalone is solid.
- **No cloud library objective.** Out of scope for the entire first quarter.
- **No quantitative DSP-quality objective beyond "author-judged usable."** Tone judgement is subjective; a numeric tone score would be theatre.

If any of these become priorities later, they get added to a future quarter's OKRs, not retrofitted here.

## Review cadence

- **Weekly:** mark KR progress (on track / at risk / blocked) in this file's changelog.
- **End of quarter:** retrospective. Move achieved KRs to a closed section; carry incomplete ones into Q2 with explicit reasoning.
